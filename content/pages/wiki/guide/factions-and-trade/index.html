Reputation and status

Each faction keeps a reputation number for you. Status is the rank that reputation has earned, from -3 to 3.

  • Status 1 needs reputation 1, status 2 needs reputation 3 and status 3 needs reputation 6.
  • Status -1 starts at reputation -1, status -2 at reputation -3 and status -3 at reputation -6.
  • Every faction starts a run at reputation 0, except Gaia, which starts at -2, and Leviathan, which starts at -1.

The game shows status as a number with a sign, such as +1, next to a face. Hovering a faction's banner at one of its places shows Status, Reputation and the reputation at which the next status begins. Every change of reputation is announced with a notification.

Discovering a faction

A faction is discovered the first time you land at a place where one of its heralds lives. The Merchants have no herald, so they are discovered the first time you open Trade with a Merchant or a Caravan. Discovery shows "Faction discovered!" and adds the faction to the Bestiary, where it stays for later runs.

What changes reputation

  • Choosing Attack on a faction's crew costs 1 reputation with that faction. Defending against a crew that attacked first costs nothing.
  • Fighting Gaia or Leviathan crews never changes reputation, whoever starts the fight.
  • Winning a battle fought beside a faction's crew gives 1 reputation with that faction, if at least one member of that crew is still alive.
  • Fleeing a battle that you joined on a crew's side costs 1 reputation with that crew's faction.
  • Every 50 gold spent buying from a Merchant or a Caravan gives 1 reputation with the Merchants. Gold left over counts toward the next 50.

What standing does

  • A faction's crews treat you as hostile while its reputation with you is below 0, and each point below 0 makes the crews it fields stronger.
  • Talk makes a crew answer with a line picked by reputation. At reputation 0 or higher, the crew also marks on your chart the nearest of its faction's sites that you have not found, once for each crew.
  • Pirates crews come after a crew that carries 50 or more gold while reputation with the Pirates is 0 or lower. Reputation above 0 stops this.
  • Status opens the higher tiers of a faction's shops, and more of its races for later runs: see Your crew.

Places to land

A location's window shows Buy if the place sells, Sell if it buys, Brew if it has a cauldron and Recruit if it has crewmates for hire. Each location's page lists what it sells, what it buys and who is for hire there.

  • Alchemist Tower, Baba Yaga, Shaman Hut and Mer Tower sell elixirs and have a cauldron. They belong to no faction, so nothing they sell is locked by status.
  • A Merchant ship on the water and a Caravan on land are moving shops. Trade opens a Market with Buy and Sell, and they buy equipment.
  • A hut only shows a description.

Prices

Every item has a cost, in gold unless the item is priced in gems. Elixirs, tonics, the three mushrooms and Hearty Stew are priced in gems. An item's page shows its cost under VALUE.

  • A place that buys cargo pays an item's full price. Any other buyer pays half, rounded down.
  • A place that buys cargo lists what is in the ship's hold. A place that buys equipment lists what is in the crew's inventory. Several items can be picked and sold together.
  • Shryke Village and Shryke Port trade in food instead of gold: a gold price is halved and rounded down, with a minimum of 1 food. Gem prices stay the same.

Shop stock and status

A faction's Market keeps its wares in tiers. Tier 0 is for anyone, and tiers 1, 2 and 3 need status 1, 2 and 3 with that faction. Wares above your status stay on show with a lock over them, and hovering one shows the faction and the status it needs.

  • While your status with a faction is below 0, its Market locks everything except resource items such as Food.
  • Food and Rum are sold from a counted stock: 12 Food and 3 Rum at most places, and 25 Food at larger ones such as Whaling Port. At 0 the game says "No stock left, come back next week".
  • Every other ware is a single item that leaves the shop when it is bought.
  • A Merchant sells Food and Rum plus 4 random goods in tier 0, 3 in tier 1 and 2 in tier 2, unlocked by status with the Merchants.

Blubber and other cargo

Blubber is cargo priced in gems. A place that buys cargo pays 1 gem for a Blubber, 2 for a Zypher Blubber, 3 for a Titan Blubber and 4 for a Mystical Blubber. A creature drop such as Silk sells for 15 gold there.

Brewing

Brewing at a cauldron takes one creature drop from the ship's hold and 1 gem, and gives one elixir or tonic, which goes to the crew's inventory. All four cauldrons use the same recipes. Each drop's description names what it brews, and its page links to it under BREWS INTO.

Gathering

  • Berries give 7 to 12 food.
  • A Gold Deposit gives 10 gold.
  • A patch of Blue, Red or Yellow Mushrooms gives one mushroom of that colour.

A gathering spot is used up: it leaves the map once you accept what it gives.
